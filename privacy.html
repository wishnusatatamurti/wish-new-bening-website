Privacy Policy

Privacy-conscious by design. Here’s what the initial Wish New Bening website does with information in your browser.

Website policy draft · Updated November 16, 2026
This policy describes the initial static website included in this prototype. The Android application has its own local SQLite storage and may require a separate, expanded policy before public release.

1. Who we are

Wish New Bening is a general wellness and breathing product developed by Muzike Indonesia. This page covers the website experience provided in this initial release.

2. Data stored locally

When you create custom breathing patterns or finish a breathing session on this website, the website saves related information in your browser’s local storage. This may include pattern names, durations, breathing phase timings, session dates, session length and a limited session history.

This information stays within that browser profile under normal operation; the website does not transmit it to an account or synchronize it with your Android app.

3. Accounts and analytics

This initial website does not include registration, sign-in, analytics scripts, ad tracking, third-party advertising, or cloud synchronization. Its design assets, styles, and functionality are supplied locally with the site files.

Wherever the site is hosted, the hosting service may separately process standard connection logs (such as IP address and request details) under its own policies. This is not controlled by the browser-local breathing feature.

4. Cookies and browser storage

The breathing and progress features use browser local storage, not tracking cookies. Deleting your browser’s site data, using a different browser, or changing devices may remove your web practice history and saved patterns.

5. Data sharing

The initial site contains no code to upload or share your breathing pattern or progress data with Muzike Indonesia or third-party services. This description should be reviewed if new features are added.

6. Your choices

You can use the informational pages without creating breathing records. You can delete individual custom patterns within Breath Studio. To remove all data saved by this website, clear the site’s storage in your browser settings. This will also remove your web session progress.

7. Children and general wellness

The site offers general wellness information, not medical advice. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of breathing exercises where appropriate.

8. Future changes

If account features, online audio, payments, analytics, third-party embeds, or additional data processing are introduced, this policy should be updated before those features launch. The effective date and contact channel should also be finalized before public deployment.

9. Contact

This prototype does not yet include a published privacy contact address. The official website must add a verified contact channel before production release.
